What's new

What changed in StyloPlan, update by update, newest first.

26 OCT 2026

Take out what the render added, by hand

  • NewRemove a spot, under Check this render in Studio: drag a box over anything the render added that your plan does not have, or paint over it with the brush, then press Fill. It is filled from the floor around it, free, and Undo puts it back. It works on a phone too: tap the spot first.
  • BetterA larger shape the check marks now offers Remove by hand, where you mark it yourself, instead of only Render again.
  • BetterWhen a spot is part of the drawing you confirmed, Remove a spot keeps it and asks. If your plan does not have it, press Take it out anyway.
  • BetterThe 3D view, Review and Studio open faster: each page is now less than half the size it was.
24 OCT 2026

Keep your earlier renders, and places to look

  • NewPlaces to look: in Studio's Check this render panel, orange rings mark anything the render drew that your confirmed drawing does not show, such as a shower or a water heater. Compare slides your drawing over the spot, and Remove it takes a small invented fixture out, free, matched to the floor tiles around it, with Undo. The rings never appear in a download.
  • NewA new render no longer replaces the one before it. In Studio, Renders lists the last three of each floor and look; pick the one you like best and your downloads and 3D use it. Switching is free.
  • BetterPricing now says what a rejected render costs (nothing, it is redone) and how long a 3D link stays up (as long as your account, with no hosting fee).
20 OCT 2026

Rooms you can read in 3D, full screen, and a simpler Review

  • NewOn your website and on a shared link, a buyer taps a room in the 3D and the view glides to it, with its name and size large enough to read on a phone. Arrows step from room to room.
  • NewFull screen: the 3D on your website and on a shared link has a button above the zoom that fills the screen with the model; the X gives it back. Code you pasted before gets it too. On an iPhone it opens the model on its own page.
  • BetterWith a keyboard: Skip to content on every page goes past the header to the work, and the upload area takes Tab and Enter, so a plan can be chosen without a mouse.
  • BetterThe Differences switch is gone from Review: it could mark places that were fine. To check the drawing against your plan, press Check against my plan in the step No extra walls or doors, and use the Compare handle.
19 OCT 2026

Porch posts, and a shared 3D that matches yours

  • NewPosts, under Windows and doors in the 3D view: the posts on a porch or deck, read from your plan. Remove one, put it back, or add one where your plan draws it.
  • New+ SUITE: in Review, say the home has a suite, and the spec line prints “+ SUITE” after the bedrooms.
  • NewHelp covers the new Posts list, updating the 3D on your website, and what you see at checkout.
  • BetterDoors in 3D: the short stretch of wall beside a doorway now stands, and each door hangs from its own wall.
  • BetterA light home loads on a light screen, and its picture on your website has a light button to match.
  • BetterA slow turn of the model no longer springs back while you are still holding it.
  • FixedA shared 3D now has the same walls and stairs as your own 3D view. For a home you shared before, press Update the version on my website once.
17 OCT 2026

Fix the drawing with its own lines

  • NewCopy a door or window: click one that is drawn right, then click the wall where one is missing. It is the drawing's own symbol, turned to fit the wall, with Flip and Nudge to set it exactly.
  • NewDifferences marks, in one colour, where our drawing and your plan differ: a line we added, or a wall on your plan we left out. Places to look, not faults.
  • NewTroubleshoot, inside Help: common problems and what to do about them, with the page you are on first, and one search for everything.
  • BetterClose a wall fills a gap with the wall beside it, line for line, on straight and diagonal walls alike.
  • BetterEscape puts down whichever drawing tool is in your hand.
  • FixedCheck against my plan now appears on a floor you have already confirmed, and checks the drawing as you corrected it.
12 OCT 2026

Studio starts where your plan wants it

  • NewStudio opens on the format that suits your plan's shape: landscape for a wide plan, portrait for a tall one, square otherwise. You can still switch.
  • BetterThe Render button gently pulses until a floor is rendered, so the one paid step is easy to find. Render again is quieter.
  • Better"Not rendered yet" now sits in Credits and notes instead of over your drawing.
11 OCT 2026

A cleaner Studio and Review

  • BetterStudio's before/after handle stays where you leave it, and the rendered side keeps its labels, so you can check names on either side.
  • BetterNo more watermark across a drawing that is not rendered yet.
  • BetterThe info panel in Review and Studio floats over the stage, the same on both pages.
  • BetterOne loading bar across the app, so a wait looks the same wherever it happens.
  • BetterOn a phone, Review shows your plan first, then the steps.
  • FixedTracing a plan again no longer pushes the plan down the page.
  • FixedStray dots and dashes left behind where text is covered are cleaned up.
  • FixedA shared 3D page opens in your plan's look from the first moment, without a white flash.
  • FixedA brand change now reaches every published floor of a project, not only the one you edited.
10 OCT 2026

Cleaner renders, faster pages, Help in the app

  • NewA new styling model: cleaner renders, and each plan is rendered at its own size and shape.
  • NewHelp opens inside the app: the steps for the page you are on, common questions and a search.
  • NewIn the 3D view, click a door or window on the model to find it in the list, add one in a doorway that was left open, and choose sliding, pocket or bifold doors.
  • BetterEvery page opens faster. The 3D view is ready in about 3.4 seconds on a phone, down from 5.5.
  • BetterThe 3D view reads more doors and windows from the drawing, including closet sliders, bifolds, pocket doors and doors onto a deck. You still check each one.
  • BetterThe app now has the same look as this site.
  • FixedDeleting a floor that had been shared works again.
6 OCT 2026

Truer walls in 3D

  • BetterThe short walls between two windows or doors are built, where before they could go missing.
  • BetterWall corners in the 3D view stay square instead of rounding off.
5 OCT 2026

Review, redesigned

  • NewReview puts its tools in a column on the left and your plan on one stage, with a handle to compare your plan with our drawing.
  • BetterA label you place sits in the same spot on every picture of the plan.
  • BetterLong room names can wrap, and labels land on their own words.
4 OCT 2026

Sheets with several plans, a kit per project

  • NewUpload a sheet with several plans on it, and each plan is found and outlined so you can pick the floor you want.
  • NewEach project keeps its own brand kit, colours and font.
  • BetterA calmer Studio preview, simpler project cards and a 3D page with fewer panels.
30 SEP 2026

Rename anything, railings that climb

  • NewRename a floor from its row, and a project from its menu.
  • BetterIn 3D, railings follow the stairs up and down and guard the open edges of a raised deck.
  • BetterThe 3D view zooms in to the scale of the house, not further.
