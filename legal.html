Terms and Privacy

LAST UPDATED 5 SEPTEMBER 2026

Plain language on purpose. If something here is unclear, that is our fault and we would like to know.

The short version. StyloPlan restyles a floor plan you already have into a presentation image. It transcribes the room names and dimensions from your drawing and asks you to confirm them; it does not measure, calculate or certify anything. The output is marketing material. It is not a construction document, and it must not be used as one.

Terms of service

1. What you are buying

A credit renders one floor of one plan in one look. Preparing a floor (tracing it, listing its rooms, correcting what we got wrong) costs nothing. Changing your brand colours, logo, typography and footer on a finished render costs nothing. Exporting the three formats and the 3D view from a render you have already paid for costs nothing. Switching a floor between the Light and Dark looks is a second render and costs a second credit, because it is a second picture.

Credits do not expire. They are not refundable for cash, and they are not transferable between accounts.

2. What we do not claim

This is the part that matters most, so it is not buried.

  • Dimensions are transcribed, never calculated. Every number that appears on a finished image was read off the drawing you supplied and shown to you for confirmation before anything was rendered. We do not derive a dimension from geometry, and we never invent one.
  • We do not verify that your drawing is correct. If the plan you upload has a wrong dimension on it, the image will carry that wrong dimension faithfully.
  • Confirmation is yours. The step where you approve the room list and the dimensions is the step that makes the output usable. We describe our process as verified (reviewed and confirmed by you) and never as guaranteed. Nothing on this site or in the product is a guarantee of accuracy, area, or fitness for any particular purpose.
  • Not for construction. Output is presentation material. It is not to be used for construction, permitting, survey, appraisal, or as the basis of a contract of sale. Every finished image carries a disclaimer line saying so, and that line is on by default.
  • Images are generated by a model and are not deterministic. The same plan and the same settings can come back looking different. We run automatic checks on every render and re-run it free when our own checks reject it, but we cannot promise a particular result.

3. Your drawings stay yours

You keep every right you had in the plans you upload. You grant us only the permission we need to run the product for you: to store your files, to send them to the image service described below, and to show the results back to you. We do not use your plans or your renders to train anything, we do not publish them, and we do not show them to other customers.

You keep the rights to the images StyloPlan produces from your plan, and you may use them commercially. That is what they are for.

You are responsible for having the right to upload what you upload. Do not send us a drawing you are not permitted to send.

5. Accounts and acceptable use

You need an account to render. Keep your sign-in details to yourself; anything done from your account is treated as done by you. Do not attempt to extract our prompts or models, to resell access to the API, or to run automated volume through the product outside the credits you have bought. We may suspend an account that does.

You can stop using StyloPlan at any time and ask us to delete your account. We may change these terms; if a change is material we will say so on this page and update the date at the top. Continuing to use the product after that is acceptance of the change.

Privacy

What we hold

Only what the product needs to work. Specifically:

  • Your account: the email address you sign in with. If you use Continue with Google, we receive your email address and name from Google; we do not receive or store your Google password.
  • Your profile and brand kit: your name and company name if you enter them, plus your logo, colours, typeface and footer text.
  • Your work: the projects and floors you create, their names, the room names and dimensions confirmed by you, and the images: your uploaded plan, the tracing made from it, and the renders.
  • Your credits: a balance, and a line for every credit spent or returned, so that a question about your account can be answered.

We do not use advertising cookies, we do not run third-party analytics or trackers on this site, and we do not sell or share your data with anyone for marketing.

Who else sees a plan you upload

Two companies, both acting for us and neither of them free to do anything else with it:

  • Supabase hosts the database, the sign-in system and the file storage. Your files sit in a private bucket that only your own account can read.
  • Google operates the image model. Your floor plan is sent to Google's Gemini API so that it can be traced and styled. This is how the product works, and there is no version of it that does not. Google's API terms state that content submitted through the paid API is not used to train their models. If sending a drawing to a third-party service is not acceptable for a particular plan, do not upload that plan.

Nobody else receives your plans or your renders.

Where it is, and for how long

Data is held in Supabase's infrastructure. We keep your work for as long as your account exists. Delete a project and it goes; ask us to delete your account and everything above goes with it, other than the minimum billing record we are required to keep.

Your rights

You can ask for a copy of what we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it. Ask from the email address your account is registered with and we will act on it. If you are in a place with statutory data rights, such as the UK, the EU, Canada or California, those rights apply and this section does not limit them.

Children

StyloPlan is a business tool and is not intended for anyone under 18.
