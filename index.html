Before you start

Short answers to what a builder asks before the first plan.

You do not have to take our word for it. When we open, try it on your own plan; the first floor is free.

How accurate are the dimensions?

They are copied from your drawing, never measured or calculated. You check and confirm each one before anything renders, and the image says so in its footer.

What if the tracing gets something wrong?

You fix it on the review screen, for free. Rename a room, correct a dimension, remove anything the tracing added, or trace the plan again. Nothing is spent until you render.

What kind of plan works best?

The plan itself: the PDF or export from your architect, not a photo or a scan. PDF, PNG, JPG and WebP all work; from CAD, such as a DWG, print the sheet to PDF. Images need a short side of 800px or more.

Can I use the images for a permit or for construction?

No. They are presentation material for marketing, and every image says so in its footer. Use your architect’s drawings for anything built or approved.

What does the 3D view show, and what does it cost?

Walls, windows, doors and stairs, read from the symbols on your drawing. Fittings like kitchen counters, appliances and the cars in a garage stay on the floor as your plan draws them, so nothing appears that your plan does not show. You confirm the windows and doors. It is built from your render, so it costs nothing extra.

What if I do not like the render?

Something the render added that your plan does not have, such as a shower or a cabinet, you can take out yourself in Studio, for free, and Undo puts it back. A render our checks reject is redone at no charge. Colours, type and logo change instantly, with no new render. Asking for a new render uses one floor from your pack.

Who owns the images, and who sees my plans?

The images are yours to use commercially. We do not train on your plans, publish them or show them to other customers. Who sees your plans lists exactly where they go.

Is there a subscription?

No. Packs are bought once and never expire, and a 3D link stays up for as long as your account does, with no hosting fee. Stop whenever you like and keep everything you made.
