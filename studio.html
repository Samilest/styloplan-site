What to check on this render

The plan is drawn by an image model, and it can differ from your drawing in small ways. Compare it with your drawing above, and look for:

  • a stair, closet or small room merged into the space beside it
  • a car drawn anywhere that is not a garage
  • a window or door that has moved, or is missing
  • anything in a room that is not on your drawing

Room names, dimensions and your branding are added by us afterwards, so those always match what you confirmed. If the plan itself is not right, Render again draws it afresh.
